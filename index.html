Grace, confidence and discipline, through Kuchipudi

We provide professional training in traditional Kuchipudi, helping students develop grace, confidence, discipline and a deep appreciation of Indian classical arts.

The academy was founded in 2007 by Guru Smt. Narapuraju Sreelatha and registered in 2008. Every class is rooted in tradition, culture and artistic excellence.

About the academy

From first steps to the stage

Students move through three levels, from posture and hand gestures to Arangetram preparation.

  1. Beginner level

    Fundamentals, posture, hand gestures and basic steps.

  2. Intermediate level

    Advanced techniques, rhythm and stage performance.

  3. Advanced training

    Professional choreography and Arangetram preparation.
